Kickoff

1:00 PM MT on KMYU & ESPN+. Weber State (5-4, 4-2 Big Sky) hosts UC Davis (5-3) after a 38-33 loss at Montana State.

Gates

Gates open 90 minutes before kickoff (11:30 AM MT). Stewart Stadium has a strict no re-entry policy.

Bags

Clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bags only (a one-gallon freezer bag such as a Ziploc works). Only clear diaper bags are allowed, and the infant must be with you when the bag is searched. Medically necessary items are allowed after inspection.

Parking

Free in all stadium lots except W4, W5 and A6. Game-day parking at the Dee Events Center is free, with a shuttle to the A6 lot from 90 minutes before kickoff until 60 minutes after the game.

Tailgating

Tailgating is in Lot W4: $10 per space, cash only, paid at the lot entrance.

Weather

Forecast from the National Weather Service as of Fri Oct 30, 12:31 AM MT: a chance of showers and thunderstorms, about 57°F at kickoff in Ogden, with a 41% chance of precipitation and a light south wind (about 5 mph).

Radio

103.1 FM. The live audio stream is linked from the Weber State schedule.

Kickoff

1:30 PM MT on FOX. BYU (6-1, 3-1 Big 12) hosts Arizona State (4-3) after a 39-31 double-overtime loss at UCF.

Gates

BYU opens all gates 120 minutes before kickoff (11:30 AM MT).

Bags

No-bag policy (not clear-bag). Only small clutch bags up to 5" x 8" x 1", with or without a strap. Medically necessary items, including diaper bags, are allowed after inspection. No outside food or drink, umbrellas or coolers.

Parking

Cougar Club reserved lots open 4 hours before kickoff (9:30 AM MT) and are held for pass holders until about the end of the 1st quarter; all parking passes are digital. State-issued disability parking is in Lot 58 (IT Building), with a free accessible shuttle from 2 hours before kickoff until 1 hour after the game.

Tailgating

Lot 18 (55 E 1230 N) opens at 8 AM, first come, first served, and stays open until 2 hours after the game. Propane and wood-pellet grills only (no charcoal or wood fires). No alcohol or tobacco anywhere on campus.

Weather

Forecast from the National Weather Service as of Fri Oct 30, 12:31 AM MT: a chance of light rain, about 58°F at kickoff in Provo, with a 32% chance of precipitation and a light south wind (about 5 mph).

Radio

Nu Skin BYU Sports Network. Flagship: KSL NewsRadio 102.7 FM / 1160 AM in Salt Lake City. Stream on the BYU Cougars app or the BYUradio app. BYU Radio on SiriusXM 166.

Kickoff

1:00 PM MT on ESPN+. It’s the Battle for the Grand Canyon: the T-Birds (3-5, 2-3 Big Sky) host Northern Arizona (6-2) after a 31-28 loss at Idaho.

Bags

Clear-bag policy: one clear bag up to 12" x 12" x 6" or a gallon-size plastic bag, plus a small clutch, purse or fanny pack up to 6.5" x 4.5". Diaper bags are allowed, subject to search.

Parking

Park only in marked stalls; improperly parked vehicles may be towed. RVs should use Lot E2 (northeast of the stadium). Lot F2 is open to all fans but not for tailgating. No overnight parking.

Tailgating

First come, first served in any unrestricted lot, starting 3 hours before kickoff (10:00 AM MT). SUU fans: Lot F4. Visiting fans: Lot G5. Thunderbird Club: north end of Lot F1. No grills or vehicles on the grass, no glass containers.

Weather

Forecast from the National Weather Service as of Fri Oct 30, 12:31 AM MT: a chance of light rain, about 52°F at kickoff in Cedar City, with a 37% chance of precipitation and a south wind around 10 mph.

Radio

Thunder 91.1 FM (KSUU) in Cedar City. Stream on WatchSports.com or KSL Sports.

Kickoff

1:00 PM MT on ESPN+. Utah Tech (1-7, 1-4 Big Sky) comes home off a 43-26 win at Cal Poly to host Idaho State (5-3).

Bags

Backpacks, bags and purses larger than 14" square are prohibited. Also not allowed: coolers of any size, glass, metal or hard plastic containers, alcohol and noisemakers. Factory-sealed plastic drink containers of 2 liters or less are allowed.

Parking

Parking is in all lots in and around the stadium and is free in student lots on game days. The lots in front of the main west entrance are reserved for Trailblazer Club members and game-day staff. First come, first served; no overnight parking. Lots close 90 minutes after the game.

The Fan Sports Network, 99.5 FM in St. George.

Game day

It’s Utah Tech’s Halloween Dark Mode Game, a Big Sky matinee at Greater Zion Stadium.

Kickoff

1:45 PM MT (3:45 PM ET) on ESPN. Utah (7-0, 4-0 Big 12) visits Cincinnati (5-2). Utah won the only previous meeting, 45-14 at Rice-Eccles Stadium on Nov. 1.

Gates

Gates open 2 hours before kickoff (1:45 PM ET / 11:45 AM MT). Nippert Stadium is cashless, and re-entry is not allowed.

Bags

Clear-bag policy: one clear bag (a 1-gallon Ziploc-style bag or a clear tote up to 12" x 6" x 12") plus a small clutch or wallet about the size of a hand. No backpacks, coolers, outside food or umbrellas; two unopened water bottles are allowed. Free lockers are outside Gates B and J.

Parking

Day-of parking is in Eden Garage (3223 Eden Avenue): $25 per game, credit or debit card only, while spots last. Shuttle buses run from Eden Garage to campus before and after the game. Accessible parking is in Woodside Garage ($25, state-issued placard or plate required).

Tailgating

Cincinnati’s official tailgates are The Grid (Campus Green) and The Commons (Bearcats Commons), sold as packages and open 4 hours before kickoff (11:45 AM ET / 9:45 AM MT). The team Catwalk comes through The Grid about 2.5 hours before kickoff. UC’s football parking page says grills are not permitted in its lots.

Weather

Forecast from the National Weather Service as of Fri Oct 30, 2:00 AM MT: cloudy, about 67°F at kickoff in Cincinnati, with a 0% chance of precipitation and an east wind around 6 mph.

Radio

Flagship: ESPN 700 AM / 92.1 FM (KALL) in Salt Lake City. Stream on the school athletics site or the ESPN 700 app.

Game day

Cincinnati lists this home game as Breast Cancer Awareness, with a Wear Black theme.

Kickoff

1:30 PM MT on USA Network. Utah State (2-5) hosts Colorado State (3-4) after a 20-17 loss at Texas State.

Gates

Gates open 90 minutes before kickoff (12:00 PM MT). Maverik Stadium has a no re-entry policy.

Bags

Clear-bag policy: bags up to 12" x 12" x 6". Opaque medical and diaper bags of reasonable size are allowed only through Gates B and E. No coolers, glass, outside food or drink, umbrellas or strollers; factory-sealed clear plastic bottles (up to one liter, one per guest) are allowed.

Parking

Single-game parking is cashless: $15 in advance or $20 on game day at the F-lot entrances. Saturday game-day lots open at 8 AM. 800 East closes 4 hours before kickoff (9:30 AM MT) and 1000 North closes 2 hours before kickoff (11:30 AM MT).

Tailgating

Tailgating is only in the designated tailgate area, which opens at 8 AM on Saturday game days.

Weather

Forecast from the National Weather Service as of Fri Oct 30, 12:31 AM MT: a chance of showers and thunderstorms, about 50°F at kickoff in Logan, with a 52% chance of precipitation and a light southwest wind (about 3 mph).

Radio

Aggie Radio Network. Flagship: KSL Sports Zone (KZNS) 1280 AM / 97.5 FM (SLC). Stream on the KSL Sports app.
