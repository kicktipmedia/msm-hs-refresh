Kickoff

2:00 PM MT on ESPN+. Weber State (6-4, 5-2 Big Sky) visits Northern Arizona (8-2) at the Walkup Skydome.

Gates

Walkup Skydome doors open 90 minutes before kickoff (12:30 PM MT).

Bags

Clear-bag policy: one approved clear bag per person — a clear tote up to 12" × 6" × 12", a clear drawstring bag up to 16" × 18" when flat, or a 1-gallon clear freezer bag — plus a small clutch about 4.5" × 6.5". Medically necessary items are allowed after inspection. Factory-sealed plastic water bottles up to 51 oz (1.5 liters) are allowed.

Parking

NAU Athletics lots open 6 hours before kickoff (8:00 AM MT). Once you enter those lots, vehicles stay until the game ends. General public parking is in the San Francisco Parking Garage (paid) and lots P45, P46, P61 and P62 (free). Accessible parking is at the front of the 4-Runner lot, first come, first served.

Tailgating

Tailgating is welcome in the Skyjacks lots (4Runner, Tundra, Tacoma). The Ponderosa, NAU’s free family-friendly pregame tailgate at the southwest entrance, opens 2.5 hours before kickoff (11:30 AM MT).

Weather

Forecast (NWS), fetched Wed Nov 11, 10:11 AM MT: showers and thunderstorms likely, about 42°F at kickoff (2:00 PM MT) in Flagstaff, with a 67% chance of precipitation and a southwest wind (about 17 mph). The Walkup Skydome is indoors; this is the forecast outside the venue.

Radio

103.1 FM. The live audio stream is linked from the Weber State schedule.

Game day

It’s the Red Rock Rivalry trophy game. Northern Arizona lists this home game as Military Appreciation.

Kickoff

5:00 PM MT on FOX. BYU (7-2, 4-2 Big 12) hosts Baylor (6-3) at LaVell Edwards Stadium.

Gates

BYU opens all gates 120 minutes before kickoff.

Bags

No-bag policy (not clear-bag). Only small clutch bags up to 5" × 8" × 1", with or without a strap. Medically necessary items, including diaper bags, are allowed after inspection. No outside food or drink, umbrellas or coolers.

Parking

Cougar Club reserved lots open 4 hours before kickoff and are held for pass holders until about the end of the 1st quarter; all parking passes are digital. State-issued disability parking is in Lot 58 (IT Building), with a free accessible shuttle from 2 hours before kickoff until 1 hour after the game.

Tailgating

Lot 18 (55 E 1230 N) opens at 8 AM, first come, first served, and stays open until 2 hours after the game. Propane and wood-pellet grills only (no charcoal or wood fires). No alcohol or tobacco anywhere on campus.

Weather

Forecast (NWS), fetched Fri Nov 13, 9:27 PM MT: mostly sunny, about 45°F at kickoff (5:00 PM MT) in Provo, with a 7% chance of precipitation and a light north wind (about 2 mph).

Radio

Nu Skin BYU Sports Network. Flagship: KSL NewsRadio 102.7 FM / 1160 AM in Salt Lake City. Stream on the BYU Cougars app or the BYUradio app.

Kickoff

8:00 PM MT on ESPN+. Southern Utah (4-6, 3-4 Big Sky) visits Portland State at Hillsboro Stadium. Portland also lists Rose City Sports Net locally.

Bags

The Portland State football visitors guide says clear bags are recommended but not required, and all bags are subject to search at the gates. No bottles, cans or outside beverages, with an exception for sealed, unopened water bottles.

Parking

General parking is $10 and RV parking is $25, cash or card, at the lot entrance, operated by Hillsboro Recreation. Premium reserved parking is $25 and requires a visible pass. Entrances A and B lead into the stadium lots.

Tailgating

Tailgating is only in the designated tailgate lot ($50, visible pass required), next to Hillsboro Stadium. Pre-purchased tailgate and premium reserved parking is limited.

Weather

Forecast (NWS), fetched Wed Nov 11, 10:11 AM MT: a chance of light rain, about 52°F at kickoff (8:00 PM MT) in Hillsboro, with a 45% chance of precipitation and a light south-southeast wind (about 3 mph).

Game day

Portland State will honor its seniors before kickoff; this is the Vikings’ final home game of the season.

Kickoff

2:00 PM MT on ESPN+. Utah Tech (1-9, 1-6 Big Sky) hosts Eastern Washington (2-8) at Greater Zion Stadium.

Bags

Backpacks, bags and purses larger than 14" square are prohibited. Also not allowed: coolers of any size, glass, metal or hard plastic containers, alcohol and noisemakers. Factory-sealed plastic drink containers of 2 liters or less are allowed.

Parking

Parking is in all lots in and around the stadium and is free in student lots on game days. The lots in front of the main west entrance are reserved for Trailblazer Club members and game-day staff. First come, first served; no overnight parking. Lots close 90 minutes after the game.

Weather

Forecast (NWS), fetched Wed Nov 11, 10:11 AM MT: a chance of showers and thunderstorms, about 59°F at kickoff (2:00 PM MT) in St. George, with a 53% chance of precipitation and a west wind (about 8 mph).

Radio

The Fan Sports Network, 99.5 FM in St. George.

Game day

It’s Utah Tech’s Homecoming game. The ticket office at Greater Zion Stadium opens 60 minutes before kickoff.

Kickoff

8:30 PM MT on FOX. Utah (9-0, 6-0 Big 12) visits Arizona (7-2) at Casino Del Sol Stadium in Tucson.

Gates

Casino Del Sol Stadium gates open 90 minutes before kickoff.

Bags

Clear-bag policy: one clear plastic or vinyl bag up to 12" × 6" × 12" (or a 1-gallon clear freezer bag) plus a small clutch about the size of a hand, after a search. Medically necessary items are allowed after inspection. Backpacks, fanny packs and cooler bags are not allowed.

Parking

Campus surface lots and garages are credit-only (garages also take tap). Cat Tran shuttles run from the Park Ave. Garage starting 2 hours before kickoff and drop off on 6th Street, just south of the stadium.

Tailgating

Tailgate lots open 6 hours before kickoff. The tailgate grounds entrance closes 3 hours before kickoff. A season or game-specific tailgate pass is required on the mall grounds. Tailgating (beer and wine allowed) is also permitted in Lot 4052 with a tailgate permit or a game-day fee. No tailgating in parking garages.

Weather

Forecast (NWS), fetched Fri Nov 13, 9:27 PM MT: partly cloudy, about 60°F at kickoff (8:30 PM MT) in Tucson, with a 1% chance of precipitation and a light northwest wind (about 3 mph).

Radio

Flagship: ESPN 700 AM / 92.1 FM (KALL) in Salt Lake City. Stream on UtahUtes.com or the ESPN 700 app.

Game day

Arizona lists this home game as Military Appreciation, with a Stripe the Sol theme. Arizona asks fans to wear red.

Kickoff

7:30 PM MT on USA Network. Utah State (4-5, 3-2 Pac-12) visits San Diego State (4-5) at Snapdragon Stadium.

Gates

Stadium gates open 2 hours before kickoff (5:30 PM MT / 4:30 PM PT).

Bags

Clear-bag policy: one clear plastic or vinyl bag up to 12" × 6" × 12" (or a 1-gallon clear freezer bag), or a purse, bag or clutch up to 4.5" × 6.5". One bag per person. Bag-check lockers are available for purchase on the east side of the stadium.

Parking

Snapdragon lots open 5 hours before kickoff (2:30 PM MT / 1:30 PM PT). On-site parking is limited; buy a pass in advance and use the entry gate listed for your lot. Lots generally close 1 hour after the game, and overnight parking is not allowed.

Tailgating

Tailgating is allowed in the on-site lots, directly behind your vehicle (no extra stalls). Only natural-gas or propane grills; no charcoal. Pop-up tents are limited to Thrive, Green and Orange lots and the Warrior Walk and reserved areas of Yellow Lot.

Weather

Forecast (NWS), fetched Wed Nov 11, 10:11 AM MT: a slight chance of rain showers, about 65°F at kickoff (7:30 PM MT) in San Diego, with a 20% chance of precipitation and a west wind (about 10 mph).

Game day

San Diego State lists this home game as Military Appreciation Night.
