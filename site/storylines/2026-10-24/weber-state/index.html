This Week’s Storyline — Weber State
Week of Saturday, Oct 24, 2026

First place is on the line in Bozeman

Weber State is 5-3 and 4-1 in the Big Sky — Saturday's trip to 6-1 Montana State is the Wildcats' biggest road test of the season.

Mtn Sports Media · October 19, 2026

Weber State's Oct. 24 game is a 1:00 p.m. MT kickoff at Montana State's Bobcat Stadium in Bozeman, streaming on ESPN+ with Weber State radio on 103.1 FM. The Wildcats are 5-3 overall and 4-1 in the Big Sky, carrying a three-game win streak. Montana State is 6-1 overall and 3-1 in conference play, and is No. 3 in the latest Stats Perform FCS Top 25 (NCAA.com listing, checked Oct. 19). Weber State is ranked in that same listing at No. 22, so both teams in this game hold a spot in the current FCS Top 25. With Weber State a half-step ahead in the conference loss column, the result in Bozeman directly affects the top of the Big Sky table.

The Wildcats' latest win showed how they are closing games. On Oct. 17, Weber State rallied past Idaho State 27-20 at home: Spencer Ferguson ran for 133 yards on 16 carries and scored the go-ahead 2-yard touchdown with 1:10 left, after Idaho State had led entering the fourth quarter. Ferguson is the player to watch at Montana State — the Oct. 17 performance made him the center of the Wildcats' late-game plan, with the go-ahead score and the workload to match.

The setting raises the degree of difficulty. ESPN's game page lists Montana State at 3-0 at home this season, and Weber State is 2-2 on the road. The Wildcats' official game center also records a difficult recent history in this series, including losses in each of the last five meetings. None of that decides Saturday, but it frames what a road win in Bozeman would represent for a team that has won three straight.
